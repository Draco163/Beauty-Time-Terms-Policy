Privacy Policy

Your skincare ritual is personal. This policy explains what Beauty Time stores, what limited information our subscription provider processes, and the choices you control.

Effective July 24, 2026

Language: English · Español

Privacy at a glance

No Beauty Time account, no advertising, and no Beauty Time skincare cloud. Your routines, notes, and progress photos stay in the app’s private storage on your device. Apple and RevenueCat process limited purchase information on iOS when subscriptions are used.

1. Who we are and scope

Beauty Time is a personal skincare routine and habit organizer developed and operated by Elias Aquino. This policy applies to the Beauty Time mobile application and the legal pages hosted at this website.

Beauty Time is designed to work without requiring you to create an account. We do not sell personal information, display third-party advertising, or use skincare information for cross-app tracking.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you choose, Beauty Time may store the following information locally:

  • Your chosen display name, age, and how your skin feels.
  • Morning and evening routines, products, schedules, timers, and reminder preferences.
  • Routine history, completion records, streaks, notes, and progress entries.
  • Profile and skin progress photos you take or select.
  • Language, haptic, notification, and other app preferences.

This information is kept in the app’s private storage. Beauty Time does not operate a server that receives or lets us view this content.

3. Camera, photos, and notifications

Camera and photo-library access are optional and requested only after you choose to take or select a picture. Selected images are copied to the app’s private storage for the feature you requested. Beauty Time does not request microphone, location, or contacts access.

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Beauty Time does not use remote push notifications to send your routine content to a server.

4. Purchases and RevenueCat

On iOS, Apple processes subscription payments. Beauty Time does not receive your payment-card or bank-account details.

We use RevenueCat to retrieve subscription offers, validate Apple receipts, unlock Premium features, restore purchases, prevent fraud, and understand subscription performance. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app-user or installation identifier, purchase history and subscription status, Apple receipt information, technical device and app information, approximate country derived from an IP address, and the last time the app contacted its service.

We do not send RevenueCat your name, age, routines, notes, photos, product list, or skin information. We do not use RevenueCat information for advertising or cross-app tracking. RevenueCat acts as our service provider; its own practices are described in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you delete individual entries, use Settings → Your data on this device → Clear data and start again, or uninstall Beauty Time. The in-app clear-data control removes the local app state, photos stored by Beauty Time, and scheduled reminders.

Clearing local data does not erase Apple’s purchase history or cancel an active subscription. Purchase history may be needed to restore access. To request deletion of information that RevenueCat processes on our behalf, contact us. We may ask for the minimum technical information needed to locate the correct anonymous identifier.

6. Backups and security

Beauty Time uses private app storage and encrypts its fast local store on supported native platforms. On Android, app backups are disabled. On iOS, operating-system backup behavior is controlled by Apple and your device settings.

No storage method is completely secure. Protect access to your device, use a device passcode, and keep the operating system current.

7. Children

Beauty Time is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children on servers operated by Beauty Time. If you believe a child sent information to us through support, contact us so we can delete it.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to information that we process. Most Beauty Time information exists only on your device and can be reviewed or erased directly in the app.

You can deny or withdraw camera, photo-library, and notification permissions in your device settings. You can manage or cancel an Apple subscription in Apple Settings. Contact us for questions or requests involving information processed by a service provider on our behalf.

9. International processing

When RevenueCat processes subscription information, that information may be handled in countries other than your own under RevenueCat’s contractual, security, and legal safeguards. Beauty Time’s local skincare content is not transferred to RevenueCat.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, its providers, or applicable law changes. We will publish the revised effective date on this page and communicate material changes through the app or its store listing when appropriate.
